# GF Swing Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

GF Swing respects your privacy. The app does not require an account and does not collect, track, sell, or share personal information.

## Camera and Microphone

GF Swing requests access to the camera and microphone only to record golf swing videos with sound.

## Video Storage

Recorded videos are stored locally on the user’s device. GF Swing does not upload videos to an external server.

## Analytics and Tracking

GF Swing does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking technologies.

## Data Collection

GF Swing does not collect personal data.
