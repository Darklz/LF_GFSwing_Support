# GF Swing Support

GF Swing is a hands-free golf swing recording and instant replay app for iPhone.

## Getting Started

1. Allow camera and microphone access.
2. Choose the countdown, recording time, and replay speed.
3. Tap **START LOOP**.
4. Perform your swing after the countdown.
5. Review the recorded clip automatically.

## Common Issues

### Camera or microphone is unavailable

Open **iPhone Settings → GF Swing**, then enable Camera and Microphone access.

### No countdown voice

Check the iPhone volume and confirm that Voice Countdown is enabled in the app settings.

### Saved clips

GF Swing stores the nine newest clips locally on your device. Removing the app will also remove its locally stored clips, so share important clips before uninstalling.
